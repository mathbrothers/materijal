Limes – potpuno školski preko tabličnih limita

Zadatak
\[ \lim_{x\to0}\frac{\sqrt[7]{\cos 2x}-1}{\ln(1-\sin 2x^2)}. \]
Ideja

Koristimo osnovne tablične limite:

\[ \lim_{t\to0}\frac{(1+t)^\alpha-1}{t}=\alpha, \qquad \lim_{t\to0}\frac{1-\cos t}{t^2}=\frac12, \] \[ \lim_{t\to0}\frac{\sin t}{t}=1, \qquad \lim_{t\to0}\frac{\ln(1+t)}{t}=1. \]
Rešenje

Najpre brojilac zapišemo u obliku pogodnom za prvi tablični limit:

\[ \sqrt[7]{\cos2x}-1 =(\cos2x)^{1/7}-1 =\left(1+(\cos2x-1)\right)^{1/7}-1. \]

Rastavimo ceo izraz:

\[ \frac{\sqrt[7]{\cos2x}-1}{\ln(1-\sin2x^2)} = \frac{\sqrt[7]{\cos2x}-1}{\cos2x-1} \cdot \frac{\cos2x-1}{x^2} \cdot \frac{x^2}{-\sin2x^2} \cdot \frac{-\sin2x^2}{\ln(1-\sin2x^2)}. \]

Prvi faktor:

\[ \frac{\sqrt[7]{\cos2x}-1}{\cos2x-1} = \frac{\left(1+(\cos2x-1)\right)^{1/7}-1}{\cos2x-1} \longrightarrow \frac17. \]

Drugi faktor:

\[ \frac{\cos2x-1}{x^2} = -\frac{1-\cos2x}{x^2} = -4\frac{1-\cos2x}{(2x)^2} \longrightarrow -4\cdot\frac12=-2. \]

Treći faktor:

\[ \frac{x^2}{-\sin2x^2} = -\frac12\frac{2x^2}{\sin2x^2} \longrightarrow-\frac12. \]

Četvrti faktor:

Stavimo \(t=-\sin2x^2\). Tada \(t\to0\), pa

\[ \frac{-\sin2x^2}{\ln(1-\sin2x^2)} = \frac{t}{\ln(1+t)} \longrightarrow1. \]

Prema tome,

\[ L= \frac17\cdot(-2)\cdot\left(-\frac12\right)\cdot1 = \frac17. \]
